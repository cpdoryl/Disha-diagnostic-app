================================================================================
STAKEHOLDER ROLES & ACCESS
================================================================================

SuperAdmin
  Access: All schools, all data
  Permissions: Full CRUD on all collections
  Audit: Can read audit logs

School Admin / Principal
  Access: Own school only
  Permissions: Create/read/update assessments and plans
  Cannot: Delete or modify audit logs

Teachers
  Access: School info (read), own assessments
  Permissions: Contribute responses, view assessments
  Cannot: Modify plans or access other teachers' data

Parents
  Access: Own child's data, school info (read)
  Permissions: Read school data, view child progress
  Cannot: See other students or school operations

Students
  Access: Own progress, school info (read)
  Permissions: Read own scores and school data
  Minimal access for privacy
