## 🤖 First Opinion Engine v3

### What Is It?

**AI-Powered School Health Prediction System**

Combines leadership perception with operational reality to provide:
- Institutional health score (0-100)
- Early warning flags
- Actionable recommendations
- 90-day predictions

### How It Works

#### **Step 1: Challenge Questions (8 min)**
```
Answer 15 targeted questions across 5 domains:

Domain 1: Leadership & Vision (3 questions)
  - Vision clarity and strategic planning
  - Change management capability
  
Domain 2: Teaching Excellence (3 questions)
  - Instructional quality
  - Student engagement and assessment
  
Domain 3: Infrastructure (3 questions)
  - Physical and technological resources
  - Maintenance standards
  
Domain 4: Support Services (3 questions)
  - Student welfare and counseling
  - Special needs support
  
Domain 5: Community (3 questions)
  - Parent engagement
  - External partnerships
```

#### **Step 2: Calculation (Automatic)**
```
S_sub Score: Leadership perception
  - Based on your answers
  - Scale: 0-100
  - Represents: What leadership thinks

M_obj Score: Operational reality
  - Based on school metrics
  - Scale: 0.2-1.05
  - Represents: What actually happens

Health Index: Final score
  - Formula: (S_sub × M_obj) - Delusion Penalty
  - Scale: 0-100
  - Represents: True institutional health
```

#### **Step 3: Results**
```
Overall Health Score (0-100):
  🟢 80-100: Excellent condition
  🟡 60-79:  Good, improvement needed
  🟠 40-59:  Fair, urgent action required
  🔴 0-39:   Critical, immediate intervention needed

Quadrant Classification:
  Quadrant 1 (Green): High perception, high reality ✅
  Quadrant 2 (Orange): High perception, low reality ⚠️
  Quadrant 3 (Yellow): Low perception, low reality 🟡
  Quadrant 4 (Red): Low perception, high reality 🔴
```

### Key Features

#### **Early Warning Flags**
```
Automatic Detection:
  ✅ Leadership delusion (perception >> reality)
  ✅ Opportunity areas (reality > perception)
  ✅ Infrastructure gaps
  ✅ Support service weaknesses
  ✅ Engagement problems
```

#### **Predictive Analytics**
```
90-Day Forecasts:
  ✅ Predicted improvement/decline
  ✅ Trend direction
  ✅ Confidence levels
  ✅ Recommendation priorities
```

#### **Actionable Recommendations**
```
Customized Guidance:
  ✅ Top 3 priority areas
  ✅ Quick wins (do first)
  ✅ Medium-term improvements
  ✅ Long-term strategic actions
  ✅ Resource requirements
```
