## 🤔 General Questions

### What is DISHA?

**Answer:**
DISHA (Diagnostic Institutional Strength & Health Assessment) is an online platform that helps schools evaluate their institutional health across 14 key dimensions by collecting feedback from multiple stakeholders (teachers, parents, students, administrators).

Think of it as a "health checkup" for your school - you get honest feedback about strengths and areas for improvement.

### Who created DISHA?

**Answer:**
DISHA was developed by the School Diagnostic Team as a comprehensive assessment solution based on best practices in educational evaluation and institutional management.

### How is DISHA different from other survey tools?

**Answer:**
```
DISHA is:
✅ Built specifically for schools
✅ Covers 14 interconnected dimensions
✅ Multi-stakeholder (teachers, parents, students, admin)
✅ AI-powered with First Opinion Engine
✅ Real-time analytics and dashboard
✅ Automated report generation
✅ Trend analysis capabilities
✅ Easy to use - no training required
```

### What schools can use DISHA?

**Answer:**
DISHA is designed for:
```
✅ All school levels (primary, secondary, senior)
✅ Government schools
✅ Private schools
✅ International schools
✅ Schools in any country
✅ Any school size (5 to 5,000 students)
```

### Is there a cost for DISHA?

**Answer:**
DISHA is currently **free** during the pilot phase. Check with your school administrator for current licensing.

### What languages does DISHA support?

**Answer:**
Currently supported: **English**

Coming soon: Hindi, regional languages

## 📝 Assessment Questions

### What are the 14 dimensions?

**Answer:**
The 14 dimensions measure different aspects of school health:

```
1. Leadership & Governance       9. Quality Assurance
2. Teaching & Learning          10. Financial Management
3. Infrastructure & Resources   11. Health & Safety
4. Student Support Services     12. Equity & Inclusion
5. Community Engagement         13. Innovation & Change
6. Curriculum & Assessment      14. Student Outcomes
7. Technology Integration
8. Staff Development
```

See FEATURE_OVERVIEW.md for detailed descriptions.

### How many questions are there?

**Answer:**
**70 questions total** - 5 questions per dimension

This takes about 10-15 minutes to complete.

### Can I skip questions?

**Answer:**
**No.** All questions must be answered to submit the assessment. This ensures data quality.

However, if a question doesn't apply to you, you can:
- Select "Not Applicable" if that option is available
- Contact your school admin for guidance

### What do the rating scales mean?

**Answer:**
Most questions use a 1-5 scale:

```
1 = Poor / Strongly Disagree / Not at all
2 = Below Average / Disagree / Somewhat
3 = Average / Neutral / Moderately
4 = Good / Agree / Significantly
5 = Excellent / Strongly Agree / Completely
```

### Can I see what others answered?

**Answer:**
**No.** Your responses are:
- Anonymous (except for school admins)
- Private and confidential
- Only visible in aggregate reports
- Never attributed to individuals

### Will my answers affect my job/grades?

**Answer:**
**No!** Responses are:
- Anonymous and confidential
- Used only for improvement
- Never used for performance evaluation
- Protected by school policy

Be honest in your feedback - it helps your school improve!

## 💬 Responding to Assessment

### How long does it take?

**Answer:**
**10-15 minutes**

Each respondent spends about 1-2 minutes per dimension.

### Do I need to do it all at once?

**Answer:**
**Yes.** The assessment must be completed in one session. You cannot save and return later.

**Tips:**
- Set aside 20 minutes of uninterrupted time
- Read questions carefully
- Give thoughtful answers
- Don't rush

### What if I accidentally close the browser?

**Answer:**
Unfortunately, if you close the browser, your responses may not be saved. 

**Solution:**
- Start over when you have 20 uninterrupted minutes
- Use the same device if possible
- Ask your admin for a new link if needed

### Can I change my answers after submitting?

**Answer:**
**No.** Once you click "Submit," your responses cannot be changed.

**Before submitting:**
- Review all your answers
- Make sure the scale is consistent
- Double-check any concerns

### Can I respond multiple times?

**Answer:**
**No.** Each respondent can only respond once to an assessment.

If you need to correct something, contact your school administrator immediately.

### What if I don't understand a question?

**Answer:**
Options:
1. Read the question description (if available)
2. Go to the FAQ
3. Ask your school administrator
4. Email support@disha.school
5. Provide your best honest answer

## 📊 Dashboard & Results

### When can I see results?

**Answer:**
**Immediately after you submit!**

But results are only meaningful when enough respondents have completed the assessment:
- Target: 80%+ response rate
- Minimum: 50% response rate
- Admin will lock assessment when ready

### How are scores calculated?

**Answer:**
Scores are calculated automatically:
```
Step 1: Your responses recorded (1-5 scale)
Step 2: Grouped by dimension
Step 3: Averaged across all respondents
Step 4: Compared to benchmark
Step 5: Displayed on dashboard
```

### What does my dimension score mean?

**Answer:**
Each dimension gets an average score:

```
0-20:  Critical (needs urgent action)
21-40: Poor (needs improvement)
41-60: Average (some gaps exist)
61-80: Good (mostly working well)
81-100: Excellent (strong performance)
```

### Can I see individual responses?

**Answer:**
**For Administrators:** Yes, admins can see aggregated data and trends

**For Teachers/Parents:** No, only group results are shared

**For Everyone:** Individual responses remain confidential

### How often is the dashboard updated?

**Answer:**
**Every 30 seconds** with real-time updates as new responses arrive.

### Can I download my responses?

**Answer:**
**Yes!** Administrators can export data in:
- CSV (for Excel)
- JSON (for analysis)
- PDF (for printing)
- Custom reports

## 🤖 First Opinion Engine

### What is the First Opinion Engine?

**Answer:**
An AI-powered tool that:
- Analyzes 15 key challenge questions
- Calculates institutional health score
- Identifies early warning signs
- Provides recommendations
- Predicts future trends

It's like having an expert consultant review your school!

### How is it different from the regular assessment?

**Answer:**
```
Regular Assessment (14D):
- 70 questions
- Measures across all dimensions
- Stakeholder feedback
- Longer completion time

First Opinion Engine:
- 15 questions
- Quick health check
- Expert-level insights
- 8-minute completion
- AI-powered predictions
```

### What questions does it ask?

**Answer:**
15 questions across 5 domains:

```
Domain 1: Leadership & Vision (3 Q)
Domain 2: Teaching Excellence (3 Q)
Domain 3: Infrastructure (3 Q)
Domain 4: Support Services (3 Q)
Domain 5: Community (3 Q)
```

Each takes ~30 seconds to answer.

### How accurate are the predictions?

**Answer:**
The First Opinion Engine:
- Uses historical data and trends
- Provides probable outcomes (not certainties)
- Accuracy improves with more data
- Best used with regular assessments

**Confidence:**
- High: 80-100% confidence
- Medium: 60-79% confidence
- Low: <60% confidence

### What are "early warning flags"?

**Answer:**
Red flags that indicate:
- Leadership delusion (thinking better than reality)
- Infrastructure gaps (inadequate resources)
- Support service issues (insufficient help)
- Engagement problems (low morale)
- Quality concerns (declining standards)

**Action:** Address these urgently to prevent problems.

### What are recommendations based on?

**Answer:**
Recommendations come from:
- Dimension scores
- Stakeholder feedback patterns
- Benchmarking against similar schools
- Best practices research
- Your specific situation

## 🔧 Technical Questions

### What browser should I use?

**Answer:**
DISHA works on:
```
✅ Google Chrome (recommended)
✅ Mozilla Firefox
✅ Apple Safari
✅ Microsoft Edge
✅ Mobile browsers
```

**Tip:** Use the latest version for best performance

### What if the page won't load?

**Answer:**
**Troubleshooting steps:**

1. **Check internet:** Is WiFi/data working?
2. **Refresh page:** Press F5 or Ctrl+R
3. **Clear cache:** Browser settings → Clear cache
4. **Try different browser:** Chrome, Firefox, etc.
5. **Check URL:** Make sure link is correct
6. **Wait a minute:** Server might be busy
7. **Contact admin:** If still not working

### Why is it slow?

**Answer:**
Could be:
- Slow internet connection
- Browser with too many tabs open
- Old device/computer
- Outdated browser version

**Solutions:**
- Close other browser tabs
- Update your browser
- Use WiFi instead of mobile data
- Try a different device
- Contact support if persists

### Will it work on my tablet?

**Answer:**
**Yes!** DISHA is optimized for:
- iPad (iOS)
- Android tablets
- All tablet sizes
- Both landscape and portrait

### Can I use the app offline?

**Answer:**
**Currently:** No, you need internet connection

## 🔐 Data & Security

### Is my data safe?

**Answer:**
**Yes!** DISHA uses:
```
✅ HTTPS encryption (data in transit)
✅ Firestore encryption (data at rest)
✅ Secure authentication
✅ Automatic backups
✅ Regular security audits
✅ GDPR compliant
✅ No third-party data sharing
```

### Who can see my responses?

**Answer:**
Your responses are seen by:
```
✅ School administrator
✅ School leadership team
✅ (Only in aggregated form)

NOT seen by:
❌ Teachers (individual responses)
❌ Students (individual responses)
❌ Other respondents
❌ Third parties
❌ Public
```

### Is DISHA FERPA compliant?

**Answer:**
**Yes!** DISHA complies with:
- FERPA (Family Educational Rights)
- GDPR (General Data Protection)
- CCPA (California Privacy)
- Standard privacy regulations

Your data is protected by law.

### How long is data kept?

**Answer:**
Data is retained:
```
Active assessments: Indefinitely
Completed assessments: 7 years minimum
Archive: Available on request
Deletion: Contact support
```

### What happens to my data after school year?

**Answer:**
Your data:
- Stays in system for record-keeping
- Available for trend analysis
- Protected same as before
- Can be exported anytime
- Can be deleted on request

### Is my identity revealed?

**Answer:**
**No.** Responses are:
- Anonymous to other respondents
- Identifiable only to admin
- Never published or shared individually
- Only used in aggregate
- Protected by confidentiality
