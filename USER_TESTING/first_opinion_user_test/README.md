## 🎯 **WHAT IS FIRST OPINION ENGINE?**

**First Opinion Engine v3:**
- Annual school health checkup system
- 15 challenge questions across 5 domains
- Rapid diagnostic (5-10 minutes)
- Objective multiplier-based scoring
- Early warning system
- Predictive analytics

**5 Challenge Domains:**
1. Growth & Enrollment
2. People & Staffing
3. Academic & Wellbeing
4. Reputation & Competition
5. Operations & Finance
