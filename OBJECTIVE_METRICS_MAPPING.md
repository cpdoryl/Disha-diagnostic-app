# Objective Metrics Mapping to 14 Dimensions

## Overview
This document maps each of the 14 DISHA dimensions to objective, quantifiable school data that can be extracted from operational systems (ERP, admission records, HR systems, financial records, etc.).

**Purpose**: Enable comparison between subjective perception data (from multi-stakeholder assessments) and objective operational reality to identify perception-reality gaps.

**Date**: August 9, 2026

---

## Mapping Reference

### Category 1: Academic Excellence (4 Dimensions)

#### Dimension 1: Academic Reputation
**Definition**: How well-known and respected is the school academically?

**Objective Metrics**:
| Metric | Data Source | Calculation | Target Benchmark |
|--------|------------|------------|-----------------|
| Board Exam Pass Rate | Board records, ERP | (Total passed / Total appeared) × 100 | ≥ 85% |
| Average Board Marks | Board records, ERP | Sum of all marks / Total students | ≥ 75 |
| Distinction Count | Board records | Students scoring > 80% / Total students | ≥ 30% |
| Grade A+ Count | ICSE/CBSE results | Students in grade A1 / Total students | ≥ 25% |
| Subject-wise Performance | Subject coordinators, ERP | Average subject-wise pass rate | ≥ 90% per subject |
| Higher Education Placement | Alumni records | Students placed in tier-1 colleges / Total graduates | ≥ 60% |

**Data Collection Method**:
- Import: Board exam result reports (PDF/Excel), ERP database export
- Frequency: Annual (post-board results), real-time from ERP

**Gap Analysis**:
- Compare: Subjective perception score vs Objective academic performance index
- High perception + Low metrics = Overestimation (communication gap)
- Low perception + High metrics = Undervaluation (marketing opportunity)

### Category 2: Welfare (3 Dimensions)

#### Dimension 5: Teacher Welfare
**Definition**: How well are teachers compensated and supported?

**Objective Metrics**:
| Metric | Data Source | Calculation | Target Benchmark |
|--------|------------|------------|-----------------|
| Avg Teacher Salary | Payroll system | Average monthly salary (salary slips) | ≥ ₹ 30,000-50,000 |
| Salary vs Regional Average | Regional pay surveys | School avg salary / Regional average % | ≥ 100% |
| Leave Days Available | HR policy, records | Sanctioned annual leave days | ≥ 30 days |
| Leave Utilization Rate | Attendance records | Leave taken / Total available × 100 | 70-80% optimal |
| Health Insurance Coverage | HR records | Teachers with health coverage | ≥ 100% |
| Provident Fund Rate | Payroll system | Employer PF contribution % | ≥ 12% |
| Staff Turnover Rate | HR records | Teachers who left / Average strength × 100 | ≤ 10% (lower = better) |
| Increment Frequency | Payroll records | Average salary increase frequency | Annual or better |
| Professional Development Budget | Finance records | Training budget allocation per teacher | ≥ ₹ 5,000 per year |

**Data Collection Method**:
- Import: HR/Payroll system, Attendance management system, Finance records
- Frequency: Annual review (Mar-Apr), Monthly for monitoring

**Gap Analysis**:
- Perception of welfare: Teacher survey scores
- Objective welfare: Compensation, benefits, turnover data
- High perception + Low metrics = Relative satisfaction despite lower compensation
- Low perception + High metrics = Morale issue despite good compensation

## Data Import Format Specifications

### Excel/CSV Format
```
Metric Name | Value | Unit | Source | Data Quality |Timestamp
Board Exam Pass Rate | 82.5 | % | ERP Export | High | 2026-08-01
Average Board Marks | 72.3 | Marks | ERP Export | High | 2026-08-01
```

### PDF Report Data Extraction
- Extract tabular data from management reports
- Parse charts and convert to numbers
- OCR for non-digital reports

### ERP System Integration
- Direct API connection (Jeevan, iGnite, etc.)
- Automated data sync
- Mapping configuration per ERP

## Validation & Data Quality

### Data Confidence Levels
| Level | Source Type | Confidence | Usage |
|-------|------------|-----------|-------|
| **Tier 1 (Highest)** | Direct school system export | 95%+ | Primary analysis |
| **Tier 2 (Medium)** | Audited reports, official records | 85% | Secondary comparison |
| **Tier 3 (Lower)** | Estimates, survey data, approximations | 75% | Trend analysis only |

### Validation Rules
- Percentages: Must be 0-100
- Ratios: Must be positive
- Dates: Must be within last 12 months
- Cross-checks: Enrollment totals must match across systems
