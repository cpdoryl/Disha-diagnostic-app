## 1. Executive Summary & Institutional Value Proposition

### **What is the DISHA Diagnostic Framework?**
The **DISHA Diagnostic Framework** is an advanced institutional diagnostic, evaluation, and strategic decision-support platform engineered specifically for school leadership teams, board trustees, and educators. It provides a standardized, multi-stakeholder assessment mechanism across **14 core institutional dimensions**, translating subjective feedback into quantitative health indices, gap matrices, and predictive improvement trajectories.

---

### **How DISHA Helps Schools**
Modern school management requires more than anecdotal observations. DISHA provides a structured, data-driven framework that enables institutions to:
- **Consolidate Multi-Stakeholder Perspectives**: Unify voices from School Leaders, Educators, Parents, and Students into a single analytics engine.
- **Identify Hidden Operational Gaps**: Spot discrepancies between teacher perceptions and parent/student experiences before they evolve into systemic issues.
- **Benchmark Against Standards**: Compare performance directly against Board baselines (CBSE, ICSE, IB, State Board) and National Averages.
- **Model Strategic Investments**: Predict the impact of targeted resource allocations on school growth over 1, 2, and 3-year horizons.

---

### **Deployment Roadmap & Onboarding Strategy**
To introduce DISHA to a school community effectively, follow this 4-phase rollout plan:

```
[Phase 1: Registration] ➔ [Phase 2: Survey Dispatch] ➔ [Phase 3: Analysis & Benchmark] ➔ [Phase 4: Action & Simulation]
```

1. **Phase 1: Institutional Setup (Day 1)**
   - School Leadership registers official institution details (Name, Board Affiliation, Location, Email).
2. **Phase 2: Multi-Stakeholder Capture (Days 2–10)**
   - Dispatch survey links/access to Leaders, Educators, Parents, and Students (Express or Deep-Dive format).
3. **Phase 3: Real-Time Audit & Benchmarking (Days 11–14)**
   - Monitor live participation rates, review raw responses via the Data Retrieval Hub, and analyze Stage 2 Gap Comparison matrices.
4. **Phase 4: Strategic Roadmap & Simulation (Day 15 Onward)**
   - Utilize Stage 3 Predictive Simulation to model intervention feasibility and present data-backed action plans to governing boards.

---

### **Institutional Value Addition**
- **Data-Backed Governance**: Present objective diagnostic data to school boards, accreditation inspectors, and parent associations.
- **Targeted Resource Allocation**: Eliminate wasted capital by investing exclusively in high-gap dimensions.
- **Proactive Risk Mitigation**: Identify student well-being and psychological safety concerns early.
- **Full DPDP Compliance**: Safeguard stakeholder data with built-in consent workflows and encryption.

## 2. Exclusive School Authentication & Context Switcher

### **Overview**
Guarantees strict institutional data isolation. All survey responses, diagnostic scores, and database queries are attached exclusively to the active school profile.

### **Required Data & Inputs**
- **Official School Name** (Text)
- **Board Affiliation** (Select: CBSE, ICSE, IB, State Board, International, Other)
- **City / Location** (Text)
- **Principal / Administrator Email** (Valid Email Address)

### **Step-by-Step Guide**
- **Step 1: Access Context Switcher**: Click on the **School Profile Badge** located in the top navigation bar or header.
- **Step 2: Register / Select Institution**: Click **"Register New Institution"** or choose an existing profile from the institution list.
- **Step 3: Enter Required Metadata**: Fill in Official School Name, Board Affiliation, City, and Email.
- **Step 4: Confirm Active Context**: Click **"Set as Active School"**. The entire application dashboard will immediately filter analytics exclusively for this institution.

## 3. Dashboard & Annual Health Checkup

### **Overview**
The executive control center providing an immediate macro view of the school’s overall diagnostic health index (0–100 score), dimension radar breakdown, and top priority domains.

### **Required Data & Inputs**
- Active School Profile context.
- (Optional) Quick diagnostic ratings if executing a rapid health checkup.

### **Step-by-Step Guide**
- **Step 1: Review Macro Score**: Inspect the **Overall Health Index** badge and risk level indicator.
- **Step 2: Examine Dimension Radar**: Analyze the 14-dimension visual radar plot to spot balance across governance, pedagogy, and well-being.
- **Step 3: Inspect Priority Domain Cards**: Review high-priority deficit areas automatically flagged by the diagnostic algorithm.
- **Step 4: Trigger Disha Checkup**: Click **"Launch Disha Checkup"** to execute a rapid annual diagnostic audit.

## 4. Stage 1: Capture (Assess - 14 Diagnostic Dimensions)

### **Overview**
Captures quantitative Likert ratings and qualitative feedback from all four primary stakeholder groups across 14 core institutional dimensions.

### **14 Core Dimensions**
1. Vision, Leadership & Governance
2. Curriculum & Pedagogical Alignment
3. Digital Infrastructure & EdTech Integration
4. Culture, Climate & Psychological Safety
5. Continuous Professional Development (CPD)
6. Inclusion, Diversity & Special Needs (SEN)
7. Community, Parent & Ecosystem Engagement
8. Student Well-being & Mental Health
9. Assessment & Academic Quality Telemetry
10. Physical Safety, Hygiene & Operations
11. Financial Sustainability & Resource Allocation
12. Environmental Sustainability & Campus
13. Career Readiness & 21st Century Skills
14. Regulatory & DPDP Privacy Compliance

### **Required Data & Inputs**
- **Stakeholder Role** (Select: School Leader, Educator, Parent, Student)
- **Class / Grade Level** (e.g., Grade 1 to Grade 12)
- **Section / Department** (e.g., Section A, Science Dept, Primary Wing)
- **Dimension Responses** (1 to 5 Likert Scale for each question)
- **Qualitative Comments** (Optional text)
- **DPDP Consent Acceptance** (Mandatory Checkbox)

### **Step-by-Step Guide**
- **Step 1: Open Capture Module**: Click **"Stage 1: Capture"** from the left navigation drawer.
- **Step 2: Launch Assessment**: Click **"Launch Stakeholder Assessment"**.
- **Step 3: Select Survey Mode**: Choose **Express Diagnostic (5 Questions)** for rapid feedback or **Full Deep-Dive (14 Dimensions)** for detailed evaluation.
- **Step 4: Fill Stakeholder & Class Metadata**: Select your Role, Class/Grade, and Section/Department.
- **Step 5: Rate Dimensions & Submit**: Provide 1–5 ratings across dimension prompts, type optional feedback, accept the DPDP privacy checkbox, and click **"Submit Assessment"**.

## 5. Stage 2: Compare (Diagnose & Benchmark)

### **Overview**
Identifies competitive advantages and critical vulnerability zones by comparing the school's diagnostic scores against national averages and board baselines.

### **Required Data & Inputs**
- **Target Comparison Baseline** (Select: National Average, CBSE Baseline, ICSE Baseline, IB Benchmark).

### **Step-by-Step Guide**
- **Step 1: Access Compare Module**: Click **"Stage 2: Compare"** in the navigation menu.
- **Step 2: Choose Reference Standard**: Use the benchmark filter dropdown to select your target comparative dataset (e.g., CBSE National Average).
- **Step 3: Analyze Gap Matrix**: Review positive score highlights and variance gaps across all 14 dimensions.
- **Step 4: Evaluate Consensus Discrepancies**: Examine stakeholder alignment charts to see where parents, teachers, and leadership views diverge.

## 6. Stage 3: Simulate (Model & Strategize)

### **Overview**
An algorithmic strategy modeling tool that projects school performance trajectories over 12, 24, and 36 months based on simulated resource and effort allocations.

### **Required Data & Inputs**
- **Target Diagnostic Domain** (Select dimension to target, e.g., Digital Infrastructure)
- **Resource Allocation Level** (Slider: 0% to 100% effort/capital allocation)
- **Time Horizon** (12 Months, 24 Months, 36 Months)

### **Step-by-Step Guide**
- **Step 1: Access Simulate Module**: Click **"Stage 3: Simulate"** from the sidebar.
- **Step 2: Select Target Domain**: Pick the specific dimension you intend to improve.
- **Step 3: Adjust Allocation Sliders**: Move the effort slider to simulate capital or training investment.
- **Step 4: Analyze Feasibility & Projections**: Observe the real-time growth curve, projected score gain (+0.8 points), and algorithmic feasibility rating (High / Moderate / Low).

## 8. Data Retrieval, Live Search & 1-Click Export Hub

### **Overview**
Direct access to raw cloud database submissions with live text filtering and multi-format data export options.

### **Required Data & Inputs**
- **Search Keyword** (Text input: Search by Name, Email, Phone, Grade, Section, City, or Comment keyword).
- **Filter Tabs** (Select: All, Leaders, Teachers, Parents, Students).

### **Step-by-Step Guide**
- **Step 1: Open Data Retrieval Hub**: Locate the **School Data Retrieval & Export Hub** on the Dashboard or Monitoring view.
- **Step 2: Perform Real-Time Search**: Type any search keyword (e.g., "Grade 10" or "Parent") to filter table rows instantly.
- **Step 3: Select Export Format**:
  - Click **"Export Selection CSV"** to download only the filtered visible subset.
  - Click **"Master School CSV"** to download the complete UTF-8 database for the institution.
  - Click **"JSON Backup"** to download a structured raw JSON snapshot for cloud archives.
- **Step 4: Inspect Detailed Record**: Click any row in the table to open a modal displaying individual response scores and written feedback.

## 9. Saathi AI Strategic Advisor

### **Overview**
An integrated AI assistant trained on DISHA frameworks and educational policy guidelines to provide tailored strategic advice to school principals and administrators.

### **Required Data & Inputs**
- **User Query / Prompt** (Natural language questions regarding diagnostic scores or policy advice).

### **Step-by-Step Guide**
- **Step 1: Launch Saathi Chatbot**: Click the floating **Saathi AI Chatbot** icon in the bottom right corner.
- **Step 2: Type Strategic Query**: Ask questions such as *"How can we improve our CPD score?"* or *"What strategies work best for parent engagement?"*
- **Step 3: Review & Apply Guidance**: Receive immediate, context-aware policy recommendations tailored to your school profile.

## 10. Data Privacy & DPDP Compliance

### **Overview**
Built from the ground up to comply with India’s **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act** and global student data protection norms.

### **Key Protections**
- **Explicit Consent**: Mandatory checkbox on all diagnostic surveys.
- **Field-Level Isolation**: Multi-tenant database security separating school data strictly by institution key.
- **Data Portability**: Full JSON and CSV export capabilities at any time.
