## 1. Executive Summary & Core Vision

**DISHA (Diagnostic Inspection & Systemic Health Assessment)** is a data-driven, clinical-grade school diagnostic platform engineered by **RYL Neuroacademy**. It transforms traditional, subjective school management into an evidence-based, SLA-driven operational ecosystem.

By synthesizing India's two most authoritative educational frameworks—the **EducationWorld India School Rankings (EWISR 14 Dimensions)** and the **National Education Policy (NEP 2020 / CBSE SQAAF)**—DISHA provides school trusts, board members, and principals with a real-time 0–100 Institutional Health Index, automated root-cause triage, and operational treatment plans.

### Key Value Proposition
* **Evidence-Based Governance:** Converts qualitative educational ideals into measurable, daily operational SLAs.
* **Stakeholder Alignment:** Bridges the disconnect between parent perceptions, teacher workloads, and leadership vision.
* **Proactive Risk Prevention:** Prevents student churn, teacher attrition, and regulatory non-compliance before they impact institutional reputation or revenue.

## 2. The Structural Problem in K-12 Institutional Governance

Most K-12 institutions operate under **"Symptomatic Firefighting"** rather than **"Predictive Health Assessment"**.

### Key Friction Points in Traditional School Management:
1. **Subjective Decision-Making:** School leadership relies on anecdotal feedback rather than quantifiable operational logs.
2. **The Parent Perception Gap:** Schools invest heavily in campus infrastructure, yet parents report poor "Value for Money" due to unaddressed daily communication delays (e.g., parent app ticketing, bus tracking).
3. **NEP 2020 Compliance Lag:** While NEP 2020 mandates 50 hours of Continuous Professional Development (CPD) per teacher per year, over 65% of schools lack systemic tracking tools.
4. **Undetected Student Attrition:** High-risk dropouts and academic slippages are identified only *after* parents submit TC (Transfer Certificate) applications, resulting in irreversible revenue loss.

## 3. Foundational Standards: Integrating EWISR & NEP 2020

DISHA's diagnostic engine is built on the convergence of two nationally benchmarked standards:

### 3.1 The EWISR 14-Dimension Quality Framework (EducationWorld India School Rankings)
Developed in partnership with **C fore**, EWISR assesses K-12 school excellence across 14 holistic dimensions:
* **D01:** Academic Reputation & Rigour
* **D02:** Teacher Welfare & Development
* **D03:** Leadership & Governance Quality
* **D04:** Parent Engagement & SLA
* **D05:** Student Safety & Wellness
* **D06:** Infrastructure & Facilities
* **D07:** Co-Curricular Education
* **D08:** Individual Attention Ratio (PTR)
* **D09:** Value for Money
* **D10:** Special Needs Inclusivity
* **D11:** Community Service & Social Responsibility
* **D12:** Faculty Competence & Retention
* **D13:** Internationalism & Cultural Diversity
* **D14:** Management Vision & Growth Drive

### 3.2 NEP 2020 Reform Pillars & CBSE SQAAF Standards
NEP 2020 and CBSE’s School Quality Assessment and Accreditation Framework mandate key operational shifts:
* **Mandatory Teacher Upskilling:** Minimum 50 hours/year of certified CPD per educator.
* **Experiential & Competency-Based Learning:** Shifting from rote memorization to application-oriented pedagogy.
* **Early Student Retention & Inclusivity:** Proactive tracking of attendance and learning gaps.
* **Institutional Governance & SLA Audit:** Multi-stakeholder accountability and transparent operational logs.

## 4. DISHA Derived Diagnostic Methodology & App Feature Map

DISHA translates complex raw school data into actionable diagnostic intelligence through 6 specialized features:

1. **First Opinion Diagnostic & Symptom Triage (`Disha Checkup`)**
   * *Logic:* School heads select present operational symptoms (e.g., Parent Complaints, Teacher Fatigue, Fee Lags).
   * *Data Document Parsing:* Upload CSV operational ledgers to parse real-time data.
   * *Output:* Generates an instant 0–100 Institutional Health Index, evidence mapping badges, and critical warning flags.

2. **14-Dimension Holistic Audit Engine (`14 Dimensions Audit`)**
   * *Logic:* Evaluates performance across all 14 EWISR parameters against state and national CBSE benchmarks.
   * *Output:* Color-coded health bands (Healthy, Low Risk, Medium Risk, Critical Gap) and spider-graph analytics.

3. **360° Multilateral Stakeholder Perception Engine (`360° Surveys`)**
   * *Logic:* Triangulates feedback from Parents, Teachers, Students, and Admin Staff.
   * *Output:* Identifies hidden perception gaps (e.g., parents dissatisfied with communication vs. admin believing SLA is met).

4. **Early-Warning Retention & Attrition Engine (`Retention SLA`)**
   * *Logic:* Analyzes consecutive unexcused absences, fee payment lags, and academic performance drops.
   * *Output:* Flags students as LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, or CRITICAL dropout risks prior to mid-term loss.

5. **NEP 2020 Teacher CPD & Pedagogical Tracker (`Teacher Development`)**
   * *Logic:* Logs workshop completion hours (e.g., Podar Innovation Lab / CBSE training).
   * *Output:* Tracks compliance against NEP 2020's 50-hour mandate per teacher and department.

6. **Automated Treatment Plan Generator (`Treatment Plan`)**
   * *Logic:* Converts audit failures into prioritized Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
   * *Output:* Assigns target execution windows, leadership owners (e.g., Vice Principal, Admin Officer), and verification milestones.
