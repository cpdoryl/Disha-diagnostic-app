## ✨ Key Features

### 7-Step Process

1. **Goal Setting** - Define current state, target, timeline, budget
2. **Reverse Calculation** - Calculate required targets for each dimension
3. **Feasibility Analysis** - Assess achievability, identify risks
4. **Action Mapping** - Convert targets to specific actions
5. **Resource Allocation** - Allocate budget in 3 tiers
6. **Timeline Planning** - 12-month phased plan with milestones
7. **Execution & Monitoring** - Track progress, enable course correction

### Mathematical Engine

- ✅ Reverse calculation formulas
- ✅ 3 allocation strategies (uniform, strategic, aggressive)
- ✅ 4-band feasibility scoring
- ✅ ROI calculations
- ✅ Confidence level calculations

### Strategic Innovation

- ✅ Works backwards from goal (unique)
- ✅ Realistic target setting
- ✅ Risk identification upfront
- ✅ Resource optimization
- ✅ Milestone-based tracking
