1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORE VISION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DISHA (Diagnostic Inspection & Systemic Health Assessment) is a data-driven, clinical-grade school 
diagnostic platform engineered by RYL Neuroacademy. It transforms traditional, subjective school 
management into an evidence-based, SLA-driven operational ecosystem.

By synthesizing India's two most authoritative educational frameworks—the EducationWorld India School 
Rankings (EWISR 14 Dimensions) and the National Education Policy (NEP 2020 / CBSE SQAAF)—DISHA provides 
school trusts, board members, and principals with a real-time 0–100 Institutional Health Index, 
automated root-cause triage, and operational treatment plans.

Key Value Proposition:
  • Converts qualitative educational ideals into measurable, daily operational SLAs.
  • Bridges the disconnect between parent perceptions, teacher workloads, and leadership vision.
  • Prevents student churn, teacher attrition, and regulatory non-compliance before they impact 
    institutional reputation.


2. THE STRUCTURAL PROBLEM IN K-12 INSTITUTIONAL GOVERNANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Most K-12 institutions operate under "Symptomatic Firefighting" rather than "Predictive Health Assessment".

Key Friction Points in Traditional School Management:
  1. Subjective Decision-Making: School leadership relies on anecdotal feedback rather than 
     quantifiable operational logs.
  2. The Parent Perception Gap: Schools invest heavily in infrastructure, yet parents report poor "Value 
     for Money" due to unaddressed daily communication delays (e.g., parent app ticketing, bus tracking).
  3. NEP 2020 Compliance Lag: While NEP 2020 mandates 50 hours of Continuous Professional Development (CPD) 
     per teacher per year, over 65% of schools lack systemic tracking tools.
  4. Undetected Student Attrition: High-risk dropouts and academic slippages are identified only AFTER 
     parents submit TC (Transfer Certificate) applications, resulting in irreversible revenue loss.


3. THE FOUNDATIONAL STANDARDS: INTEGRATING EWISR & NEP 2020
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DISHA's diagnostic engine is built on the convergence of two nationally benchmarked standards:

3.1 The EWISR 14-Dimension Quality Framework (EducationWorld India School Rankings)
Developed in partnership with C fore, EWISR assesses school excellence across 14 holistic dimensions:
  • D01: Academic Reputation & Rigour          • D08: Individual Attention Ratio (PTR)
  • D02: Teacher Welfare & Development         • D09: Value for Money
  • D03: Leadership & Governance Quality       • D10: Special Needs Inclusivity
  • D04: Parent Engagement & SLA               • D11: Community Service & Social Responsibility
  • D05: Student Safety & Wellness             • D12: Faculty Competence & Retention
  • D06: Infrastructure & Facilities           • D13: Internationalism & Cultural Diversity
  • D07: Co-Curricular Education               • D14: Management Vision & Growth Drive

3.2 The NEP 2020 Reform Pillars & CBSE SQAAF Standards
NEP 2020 and CBSE’s School Quality Assessment and Accreditation Framework mandate key operational shifts:
  • Mandatory Teacher Upskilling: Minimum 50 hours/year of certified CPD per educator.
  • Experiential & Competency-Based Learning: Shifting from rote memorization to application-oriented pedagogy.
  • Early Student Retention & Inclusivity: Proactive tracking of attendance and learning gaps.
  • Institutional Governance & SLA Audit: Multi-stakeholder accountability and transparent operation logs.

4. DISHA DERIVED DIAGNOSTIC METHODOLOGY & APP FEATURE MAP
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DISHA translates complex raw school data into actionable diagnostic intelligence through 6 specialized features:

1. FIRST OPINION DIAGNOSTIC & SYMPTOM TRIAGE (In-App Feature: "Disha Checkup")
   • Logic: School heads select present operational symptoms (e.g., Parent Complaints, Teacher Fatigue, Fee Delays).
   • Data Document Parsing: Upload CSV operational ledgers to parse real-time data.
   • Output: Generates an instant 0–100 Institutional Health Index, evidence mapping badges, and critical warning flags.

2. 14-DIMENSION HOLISTIC AUDIT ENGINE (In-App Feature: "14 Dimensions Audit")
   • Logic: Evaluates performance across all 14 EWISR parameters against state and national CBSE benchmarks.
   • Output: Color-coded health bands (Healthy, Low Risk, Medium Risk, Critical Gap) and spider-graph analytics.

3. 360° MULTILATERAL STAKEHOLDER PERCEPTION ENGINE (In-App Feature: "360° Surveys")
   • Logic: Triangulates feedback from Parents, Teachers, Students, and Admin Staff.
   • Output: Identifies hidden perception gaps (e.g., parents dissatisfied with communication vs. admin believing SLA is met).

4. EARLY-WARNING RETENTION & ATTRITION ENGINE (In-App Feature: "Retention SLA")
   • Logic: Analyzes consecutive unexcused absences, fee payment lags, and academic performance drops.
   • Output: Flags students as LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, or CRITICAL dropout risks prior to mid-term loss.

5. NEP 2020 TEACHER CPD & PEDAGOGICAL TRACKER (In-App Feature: "Teacher Development")
   • Logic: Logs workshop completion hours (e.g., Podar Innovation Lab / CBSE training).
   • Output: Tracks compliance against NEP 2020's 50-hour mandate per teacher and department.

6. AUTOMATED TREATMENT PLAN GENERATOR (In-App Feature: "Treatment Plan")
   • Logic: Converts audit failures into prioritized Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
   • Output: Assigns target execution windows, leadership owners (e.g., Vice Principal, Admin Officer), and verification milestones.
